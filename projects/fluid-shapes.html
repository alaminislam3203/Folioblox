Fluid Shapes

Client

Studio Form

Category

Visual Identity

Year

2022

Live Project

View site →

Challenge

Studio Form sought a visual identity system that could express the tension between mathematical precision and human organic warmth. The branding had to accommodate an expansive physical exhibition, digital cataloging systems, and a series of experimental generative animations.

Goal

We engineered a parametric logo and design grammar where geometric primitives undergo algorithmic transformations based on mouse interactions and motion. The resulting visual language balances clean Swiss typography with dynamic sculptural forms.

Result

The brand design won multiple prestigious visual communication honors and was showcased at prominent European design biennials. The identity delivered a distinctive voice that cemented Studio Form's reputation as a cutting-edge creative powerhouse.
